# Art for the 24-sector expansion (generated 9/11/2026)

All of this art is in the repo. It was generated through the logged-in browser
profiles with `node ~/.claude/skills/translate/scripts/art.mjs` (see "Art
generation" in the translate skill): Grok Imagine for the story stills, ChatGPT
with existing sprites as style references for ships and bosses. Sprites were
cut out to transparent PNGs with `scripts/tools/cutout_sprite.py`
(`--pad 12` ships, `--pad 2` bosses, `--hole-min 999999` when a hull has
white plates or a white-hot core that must not be punched out).

To redo one image: put the prompt in a file, run the command, LOOK at the
result, cut out, re-import (`godot --headless --path . --import`).
